Deposit Funds

Deposit funds into your GrowVest wallet using M-Pesa.

How it works:
1. Enter the deposit amount.
2. Enter your M-Pesa number.
3. Tap the button to request STK Push.
4. Enter your M-Pesa PIN when prompted.
5. Wait for payment confirmation.
